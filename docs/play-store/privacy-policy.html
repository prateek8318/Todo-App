Tickd Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Tickd is a task and routine planner provided by Prateeek Pandey. This policy explains how Tickd handles information when you plan tasks, set reminders, customise the app and use its finance and AI features.

Information stored on your device

Tickd stores tasks, descriptions, priorities, due dates, repeat settings, completion information, income and expense entries, amounts, optional split names, and your app preferences on your device. This supports your task lists, reminders, budget summaries and appearance settings. Tickd does not currently provide user accounts or cloud synchronisation of these records.

Information sent for AI features

When AI is configured and requests are made, Tickd sends relevant inputs to OpenAI over HTTPS. Daily insights use the titles and priorities of pending tasks. Budget suggestions use your total recorded income, expenses and estimated split amount owed. AI task parsing sends the text you enter and the current date to interpret your task.

The Smart Dashboard requests both task insights and budget suggestions automatically when opened and when its task or finance data changes, including when its task tab is selected. AI task parsing is triggered by the parse action. These requests do not separately include your full transaction history or split names, but any personal details you put into task titles or parsing text are included in that text.

OpenAI receives these inputs and associated network information to process requests and operate its service. Its processing may take place outside your country. Avoid entering passwords, payment credentials or information you do not want processed by an AI provider. See OpenAI's privacy policy and API data controls for provider practices.

Notifications and messaging services

Tickd uses Notifee to schedule local task reminders. Task titles and reminder content may appear in your notification area or on your lock screen, depending on your Android settings. Notification and scheduling permissions support these reminders.

Tickd also includes Firebase Cloud Messaging. When Firebase is configured, its SDK may initialise and process installation identifiers, messaging registration data and technical connection information to support notification delivery. Denying notification display permission does not necessarily prevent SDK initialisation. Google's Firebase privacy information describes its processing and retention.

How information is used and disclosed

Information is used to provide task planning, reminders, local finance tracking, personalisation and AI suggestions. Data sent to OpenAI and Firebase is handled by those providers for the services described above and their applicable operational and security purposes. Tickd does not sell your personal information or include advertising integrations in its current version. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app's stored data. You can delete individual tasks and finance entries in the app. To remove all local records and preferences, open Android Settings, select Tickd under Apps, then choose Storage and clear the app's data. Uninstalling generally removes its local app storage. These actions do not recall requests already transmitted to providers.

OpenAI's standard API abuse-monitoring logs may retain request content for up to 30 days, with exceptions described in its data controls; retention depends on the applicable account settings and provider requirements. Firebase retains installation identifiers under its published practices, including deletion processes that may require a provider API request. Tickd currently has no in-app control that directly deletes OpenAI logs or Firebase installation records. Contact us for privacy questions or requests concerning provider-held information; we will assess available mechanisms and applicable obligations. We do not promise deletion of provider records merely because you uninstall.

Security and your choices

AI requests use HTTPS. Local records are held within the app's storage and rely on your device's security controls. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. You can manage notification permission and lock-screen visibility through Android settings and use Tickd's notification toggle. You can manage or delete local entries as described above. The current version does not offer a dedicated AI privacy opt-out switch.

Young users

Tickd is intended for teens aged 13 and older and adults, and is not directed to children under 13. Where local law or provider terms require a higher minimum age or parental permission, those requirements apply. If you believe information from a child has been transmitted inappropriately, contact us so we can assess the request and available deletion measures.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Tickd changes. The effective date identifies the current version. Material changes to data handling will be communicated through an appropriate notice.
