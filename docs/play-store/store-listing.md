## Short description

## Full description

Make room for what matters with Tickd. Plan today's tasks, build a repeatable routine and give your planner a look you enjoy opening.

PLAN YOUR DAY
Add tasks, choose a priority and set a due date. See today's plans in one place and mark them done as you go.

BUILD YOUR ROUTINE
Repeat tasks daily, weekly or monthly for study sessions, classes, household jobs and everyday habits.

SET REMINDERS
Choose reminder times for your tasks. Notification delivery depends on your device permissions and battery settings.

MAKE IT YOURS
Explore cute and studio wallpaper styles, with light and dark themes to match your mood.

FIND YOUR FOCUS
The Smart Dashboard brings priorities and overdue tasks into view so you can decide what to tackle next.

TRACK EVERYDAY MONEY
Record income and expenses and keep a simple view of your totals. Entries with a split name use a simple half-share estimate; Tickd does not make payments or settle debts.

START WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
Your task lists, finance entries and preferences are stored on your device. AI features, when available, use an internet connection and send relevant inputs to OpenAI. The Smart Dashboard can request task insights and budget suggestions automatically as its data changes. See the privacy policy for details.

Tickd is designed for teens aged 13 and older and adults. Budget suggestions are general information and should not be relied on as professional financial advice.
